      03 About

      Roots & rhythm

      Love Pump was born in Portugal to Angolan parents and grew up in Bergen. Her sets draw on the club cultures of Lisboa and Luanda — and on everything in between.

      In 2018 she founded Klubb Diaspora with her sister Miss Tati: a multicultural club night built around the sound of the diaspora.

      She has played Øya Festival, Borealis, Hot Hot Hot, Landmark and Jaeger, and stages in London, Montpellier and Copenhagen — and is today one of the most influential DJs in Bergen.
